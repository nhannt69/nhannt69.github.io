This paper proposes WaFIR, a Wavelet-Fourier mixture-of-experts with subband-adaptive routing that derives frequency priors from the input image to guide blind all-in-one restoration under unknown degradations (noise, haze, rain).

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29108) | [Code](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/WaFIR/README.md)

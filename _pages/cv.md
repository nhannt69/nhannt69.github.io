RESEARCH EXPERIENCE
======

**Pukyong National University, Busan, Korea**<br>
Graduate Research Assistant

Supervisor: Prof. Junghwan Oh<br>
Oct. 2023 – Present

[1] Project: **Automated Robotic Inspection of Weld Surfaces** - [Code](https://github.com/nhannt69/VIKO_UltraRobot)
- Led a small team (3 graduate students), planned the project timeline, coordinated daily tasks, and reported weekly progress to the professor.
- Integrated a Yaskawa robotic arm, a Micro-Epsilon laser scanner, and a Basler camera into an automated pipeline for weld identification and 3D surface scanning.
- Achieved 95.36% weld-identification accuracy with the vision module.
- Developed a real-time robot control system for laser surface scanning at 65 mm/s with 100 μm × 49 μm resolution, enabling precise 3D visualization of weld surfaces.

[2] Project: **AI-Based Defect Inspection for Scanning Acoustic Microscopy** - [Code](https://github.com/nhannt69/SAM-Inspection)
- Developed a U-Net segmentation model with a ResNet50 encoder to segment solder voids and chip regions in SAM images of power semiconductor modules.
- Built a sliding-window inference pipeline that segments full-resolution scans.
- Deployed the pipeline as the automated defect-inspection function in the software of [Ohlabs' commercial SAM systems](https://www.ohlabs.co.kr/company), now used by industrial customers.

[3] Project: **AI-Based MLCC Inspection System** - Client: [Samwha](https://www.samwha.co.kr/capacitor_eng/030205.asp)
- Developed an image-processing pipeline to detect abnormal multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs).
- Integrated the detected defect positions with an automated laser-marking stage.
- Trained a YOLO-based detector to verify that defective MLCCs were correctly laser-marked, covering five MLCC sizes (1005, 1608, 2012, 3216, 3225).
- Deployed the system at the customer's production site, where it remains in use and reduces the time and manual effort needed for defect detection and post-marking inspection.

[4] Project: **Image Restoration for Ultrasound Imaging** - Papers: [SAMGAN](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095219762502531X), [DIPSAM](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0045790626005677), [SAMID](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11354148)
- Started the lab's research line on AI-based SAM image restoration, which led to follow-up studies by labmates, including GAN-based super-resolution for linear R-SAM ([LinearTGAN](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/12/6780), Applied Sciences, 2025) and an M.S. thesis on out-of-focus restoration (N. D. Quan, Pukyong National University, 2026).
- Super-resolution: developed a GAN-based model that reconstructs high-resolution images from coarse-step scans, reducing scanning time by up to 47.2%; integrated as an optional function in the lab's scanning software.
- Inpainting: developed a latent diffusion framework, trained on synthetic masks, that restores regions lost to water-coupling failure and improves downstream defect segmentation.
- Denoising: developed a hybrid Transformer–Mamba diffusion model with a physics-guided synthetic data pipeline (degradation modeling and CycleGAN domain transfer) to remove depth-dependent noise without clean references.
- Published four first-author papers: IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Computers & Electrical Engineering, and IEEE ICIT 2025.

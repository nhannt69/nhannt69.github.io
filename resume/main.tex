\section*{RESEARCH EXPERIENCE}
\begin{cvlist}
    \item
    \cventry{Pukyong National University, Busan, Korea}{Graduate Research Assistant}
    \cvline{Supervisor: Prof. Junghwan Oh}{Oct. 2023 -- Present}
    \begin{enumerate}[label={[\arabic*]}, leftmargin=*]
        \item \textbf{Project: Automated Robotic Inspection of Weld Surfaces} - \href{https://github.com/nhannt69/VIKO_UltraRobot}{[Code]}
        \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
            \item Led a small team (3 graduate students), planned the project timeline, coordinated daily tasks, and reported weekly progress to the professor.
            \item Integrated a Yaskawa robotic arm, a Micro-Epsilon laser scanner, and a Basler camera into an automated pipeline for weld identification and 3D surface scanning.
            \item Achieved 95.36\% weld-identification accuracy with the vision module.
            \item Developed a real-time robot control system for laser surface scanning at 65\,mm/s with 100 \textmu m $\times$ 49 \textmu m resolution, enabling precise 3D visualization of weld surfaces.
        \end{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Project: AI-Based Defect Inspection for Scanning Acoustic Microscopy} - \href{https://github.com/nhannt69/SAM-Inspection}{[Code]}
        \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
            \item Developed a U-Net segmentation model with a ResNet50 encoder to segment solder voids and chip regions in SAM images of power semiconductor modules.
            \item Built a sliding-window inference pipeline that segments full-resolution scans.
            \item Deployed the pipeline as the automated defect-inspection function in the software of \href{https://www.ohlabs.co.kr/company}{Ohlabs' commercial SAM systems}, now used by industrial customers.
        \end{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Project: AI-Based MLCC Inspection System} - Client: \href{https://www.samwha.co.kr/capacitor_eng/030205.asp}{Samwha}
        \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
            \item Developed an image-processing pipeline to detect abnormal multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs).
            \item Integrated the detected defect positions with an automated laser-marking stage.
            \item Trained a YOLO-based detector to verify that defective MLCCs were correctly laser-marked, covering five MLCC sizes (1005, 1608, 2012, 3216, 3225).
            \item Deployed the system at the customer's production site, where it remains in use and reduces the time and manual effort needed for defect detection and post-marking inspection.
        \end{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Project: Image Restoration for Ultrasound Imaging} - Papers: \href{https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095219762502531X}{SAMGAN}, \href{https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0045790626005677}{DIPSAM}, \href{https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11354148}{SAMID}
        \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
            \item Started the lab's research line on AI-based SAM image restoration, which led to follow-up studies by labmates, including GAN-based super-resolution for linear R-SAM (\href{https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/12/6780}{LinearTGAN}, Applied Sciences, 2025) and an M.S. thesis on out-of-focus restoration (N. D. Quan, Pukyong National University, 2026).
            \item Super-resolution: developed a GAN-based model that reconstructs high-resolution images from coarse-step scans, reducing scanning time by up to 47.2\%; integrated as an optional function in the lab's scanning software.
            \item Inpainting: developed a latent diffusion framework, trained on synthetic masks, that restores regions lost to water-coupling failure and improves downstream defect segmentation.
            \item Denoising: developed a hybrid Transformer--Mamba diffusion model with a physics-guided synthetic data pipeline (degradation modeling and CycleGAN domain transfer) to remove depth-dependent noise without clean references.
            \item Published four first-author papers: IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Computers \& Electrical Engineering, and IEEE ICIT 2025.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{enumerate}
